System Development (SYS)

Weekly notes from the System Development course: what we covered each week and what I took away from it.

  1. Week34 Introduction to System Development The foundations of system development: the SDLC, Royce’s 1970 paper on the waterfall model, and the Agile Manifesto. August 17, 2026 ↗
  2. Week35 Scrum The Scrum framework: roles, events, artifacts, timeboxed sprints, Definition of Done and how Scrum supports iterative and incremental development. August 24, 2026 ↗
  3. Week36 Requirements Specification Functional and non-functional requirements, user stories and INVEST, acceptance criteria, Planning Poker, story points vs. T-shirt sizes, MoSCoW and the Definition of Done. August 31, 2026 ↗
  4. Week37 Scrum Project Kickoff Kickoff of our Scrum project with Alpha Solutions: shared understanding, the first Product Backlog, Definition of Done, GitHub setup and the Sprint Zero debate. September 7, 2026 ↗
  5. Week38 Sprint Review and Sprint Planning Our first Sprint Review and Sprint Planning with the Product Owner: demoing the increment, adapting the Product Backlog and choosing the next Sprint Goal. September 14, 2026 ↗
  6. Week39 Reflecting on Our Scrum Practice Second Sprint Review and Planning, and a deeper look at how we work: retrospectives, velocity, the Scrum board, backlogs, pull requests, acceptance criteria and sprint goals. September 21, 2026 ↗
  7. Week40 Quality Assurance and Testing Quality assurance in a Scrum project: the test pyramid, test strategy, what to automate, test case design techniques, testability and quality as a continuous part of the sprint. September 28, 2026 ↗
