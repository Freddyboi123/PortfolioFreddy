*Graphs and shortest paths.* The assignment has two parts:

1. **Dijkstra**: find the shortest route between cities in a road network with at least 8 cities and **weighted, directed** roads.
2. **A\***: find the shortest route between named cities on a grid of at least 8×8.

For both parts, I also had to make a visualization that explains *how* the algorithm works, not just what the answer is.

Code: [`src/main/java/graphs`](https://github.com/Freddyboi123/AdvancedPrograming/tree/main/src/main/java/graphs) in my AdvancedPrograming repository.
